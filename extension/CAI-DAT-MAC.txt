CAI META HELPER 2.9.0 VAO CHROME TREN MAC

1. Nhan dup file ZIP de giai nen bang Archive Utility cua macOS.
2. Mo thu muc vua giai nen: phai thay manifest.json, background.js, content.js va cac icon PNG.
3. Chuyen thu muc nay vao Documents de giu lau dai.
4. Mo Google Chrome, nhap chrome://extensions vao thanh dia chi.
5. Bat Developer mode (Che do danh cho nha phat trien).
6. Neu co ban Meta Helper cu, tam tat ban cu de tranh chay hai ban.
7. Bam Load unpacked (Tai tien ich da giai nen), chon THU MUC co manifest.json, bam Select / Chon.
8. Kiem tra the tien ich hien version 2.9.0, bat tien ich, ghim neu can.
9. Tai lai Facebook Ads Manager va website FBTQ bang Command + Shift + R.

Luu y:
- Khong chon file ZIP hay file manifest.json rieng le. Chrome dang yeu cau chon THU MUC.
- Hop thoai chon thu muc co the khong hien tep manifest.json; hay chon dung thu muc chua tep do.
- Khong chon thu muc website hoac thu muc cha cua tien ich.
- Khong xoa/di chuyen thu muc sau khi nap; neu chuyen thi nap lai tu vi tri moi.
- Neu van loi, chup thong bao trong hop "Failed to load extension" de xac dinh loi thuc te.
- Neu Chrome do cong ty quan ly va chan Developer mode, can quan tri trinh duyet cho phep cai dat; goi ZIP khong bo qua chinh sach quan ly.

Goi nay chi chua extension. Ban sua anh xa tren website van can cap nhat tu goi truoc.

Ban 2.9.0 can cap nhat website di kem de bo qua Tro ly: chi so mo Bao cao hang ngay; PDF mo Bao cao hoa don.
